# Draft review checklist

Review the verified artifacts with a qualified human before approval:

- Every KC has exact supporting content-unit evidence.
- Names, descriptions, roles, and Bloom levels are supported by that evidence.
- No important taught capability is missing.
- Similar KCs are not accidental duplicates, and distinct mastery states are not collapsed.
- Parent topics have a clear central capability and sensible boundaries.
- Singletons and cross-Ward leaf moves have defensible rationales.
- `unresolved_issues_vi` is empty or each issue has an owner and resolution.
- `run-manifest.json` still has `auto_publish: false` and `production_write: false`.

Report concerns and proposed edits separately from the generated artifacts. Approval or publication requires an explicit follow-up request.
